# Core EIC and Edge EIC

*On roots and branches.*

Everything from Attributes Dynamics through Status Reciprocity sits between two anchors: Attributes Regulation opens the journey, Attributes Synthesis closes it. That stretch is complete and integrated. Each part depends on the ones before it, and it has been checked end to end for internal coherence. We'll call the whole stretch **Core EIC**.

- **CORE EIC**: The foundational, integrated development of EIC, running from Attributes Dynamics through the close of Attributes Synthesis.

Core EIC is a floor, not a ceiling. Once the anchors are in place, a single piece of that structure can be developed further than the core trajectory strictly required — a compass direction into its own gradation, a discernment child into a fuller typology. That further work does not have to happen all at once, or evenly across every component. It can grow from one point on a stable trunk, at its own pace, without waiting for a matching development elsewhere. We'll call that **Edge EIC**.

- **EDGE EIC**: Additional EIC development extending beyond Core EIC into specific, still-evolving methods. Individual Edge EIC concepts don't need to cover every Core EIC component symmetrically, and can grow independently of one another.

The distinction is also a signal about stability. Core EIC earned its coherence the hard way: collisions caught, cross-references verified, terms no longer drifting. Edge EIC concepts are younger, narrower, and more likely to be revised, renamed, or reworked as they grow. Read them with that in mind. Operational Status, next, is the first developed Edge piece — one leaf, with more of the branch still to grow.
