# Core EIC and Edge EIC

*On roots and branches.*

Everything from Attributes Dynamics through Status Reciprocity sits inside two anchors: Attributes Regulation opens the journey, Attributes Synthesis closes it. That stretch is complete and integrated — each part depends on the ones before it, and it has been checked end to end for internal coherence. We'll call this whole stretch **Core EIC**.

- **CORE EIC**: The foundational, integrated development of EIC, running from Attributes Dynamics through the close of Attributes Synthesis.

But Core EIC is a floor, not a ceiling. Once the anchors are in place, individual pieces of that structure can still be developed much further than the core trajectory strictly required — a compass direction expanded into its own gradation system, say, or a discernment child worked into a fuller typology. This further development doesn't need to happen all at once, or evenly across every component. It grows the way a branch grows: from a specific point on a stable trunk, at its own pace, without waiting for a matching branch to appear somewhere else. We'll call this kind of development **Edge EIC**.

- **EDGE EIC**: Additional EIC development extending beyond Core EIC into specific, still-evolving methods. Individual Edge EIC concepts don't need to cover every Core EIC component symmetrically, and can grow independently of one another.

This distinction is also a signal about stability. Core EIC has earned its coherence the hard way — collisions get caught, cross-references get verified, terms stop drifting. Edge EIC concepts are younger, narrower in scope, and more likely to be revised, renamed, or reworked as they grow; read them with that in mind. The section that follows, Operational Status, is the first developed Edge EIC concept — one leaf, with more of the branch still to grow.
