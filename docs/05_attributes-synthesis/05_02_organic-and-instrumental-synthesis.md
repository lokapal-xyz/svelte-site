# Organic and Instrumental Synthesis

*On life and form.*

Organic and <plain>instrumental synthesis</plain> are closer to each other than either is to mixed synthesis, and they are simpler. We take them together.

- **Interstitial Anatomy**: `{iD ≡ iO ≡ iR ≡ iE}`

Anatomy and physiology could be joined in more than one way. They stand at equal hierarchy, so <plain>symmetry</plain> (`≡`) is a candidate. They are also distant in kind, so <plain>concatenation</plain> (`^`) is a candidate. Neither quite fits. Interstitial anatomy is sophisticated, and **lifeless**. Interstitial physiology is a strong animating force, and **formless**. A lifeless structure and a formless force do not sit beside each other as a pair, and they do not merely co-occur. The force *alters* the structure. Physiology modulates anatomy.

- **Organic Synthesis**: `[I≢L → (I≡ ^ I≢)] → {iD ≡ iO ≡ iR ≡ iE}` = oΣ
- **Instrumental Synthesis**: `[C≡L → (C≡ ^ C≢)] → {iD ≡ iO ≡ iR ≡ iE}` = iΣ

A body that discerns a threat is not Discernment in the abstract. It is <plain>discernment under a perdurability longing: Organic Discernment.</plain> A protocol that watches a metric is not Observation in the abstract. It is <plain>observation under a functionality longing: Instrumental Observation.</plain>

To mark which method is being animated, the full form accents the quadrant:

- **<plain>Discernment</plain>**: `{iD ← (iO ≡ iR ≡ iE)}`
- **<plain>Organic Discernment</plain>**: `[I≢L → (I≡ ^ I≢)] → {iD ← (iO ≡ iR ≡ iE)}`
- **<plain>Observation</plain>**: `{iO ← (iD ≡ iR ≡ iE)}`
- **<plain>Instrumental Observation</plain>**: `[C≡L → (C≡ ^ C≢)] → {iO ← (iD ≡ iR ≡ iE)}`

The full form is honest, and heavy. Because a longing and a quadrant do not meet in any other context, a shorter form does not collide:

- <plain>**Organic Discernment (Simplified Form)**: I≢L → iD</plain>
- <plain>**Instrumental Observation (Simplified Form)**: C≡L → iO</plain>

Those shortenings are the tip of what organic and <plain>instrumental synthesis</plain> can do. Their implementations can wait. Mixed synthesis cannot: it is where the two longings occupy the same <plain>system</plain>.
