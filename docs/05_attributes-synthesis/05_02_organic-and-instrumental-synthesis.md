# Organic and Instrumental Synthesis

*On life and form.*

Organic and instrumental synthesis are closer to each other than either is to mixed synthesis, and they are simpler. We take them together.

- **Interstitial Anatomy**: `{iD ≡ iO ≡ iR ≡ iE}`

Anatomy and physiology could be joined in more than one way. They stand at equal hierarchy, so symmetry (`≡`) is a candidate. They are also distant in kind, so concatenation (`^`) is a candidate. Neither quite fits. Interstitial anatomy is sophisticated, and **lifeless**. Interstitial physiology is a strong animating force, and **formless**. A lifeless structure and a formless force do not sit beside each other as a pair, and they do not merely co-occur. The force *alters* the structure. Physiology modulates anatomy.

- **Organic Synthesis**: `[I≢L → (I≡ ^ I≢)] → {iD ≡ iO ≡ iR ≡ iE}` = oΣ
- **Instrumental Synthesis**: `[C≡L → (C≡ ^ C≢)] → {iD ≡ iO ≡ iR ≡ iE}` = iΣ

A body that discerns a threat is not Discernment in the abstract. It is discernment under a perdurability longing: Organic Discernment. A protocol that watches a metric is not Observation in the abstract. It is observation under a functionality longing: Instrumental Observation.

To mark which method is being animated, the full form accents the quadrant:

- **Discernment**: `{iD ← (iO ≡ iR ≡ iE)}`
- **Organic Discernment**: `[I≢L → (I≡ ^ I≢)] → {iD ← (iO ≡ iR ≡ iE)}`
- **Observation**: `{iO ← (iD ≡ iR ≡ iE)}`
- **Instrumental Observation**: `[C≡L → (C≡ ^ C≢)] → {iO ← (iD ≡ iR ≡ iE)}`

The full form is honest, and heavy. Because a longing and a quadrant do not meet in any other context, a shorter form does not collide:

- **Organic Discernment (Simplified Form)**: I≢L → iD

Those shortenings are the tip of what organic and instrumental synthesis can do. Their implementations can wait. Mixed synthesis cannot: it is where the two longings occupy the same system.
