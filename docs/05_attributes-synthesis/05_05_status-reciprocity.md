# Status Reciprocity

*On bringing the hidden into the surface.*

Unification and conciliation are interstitial. They have intrinsic correlates — names a <plain>system</plain> already wears on the outside. That pairing is <plain>**Status Reciprocity**</plain>:

- Unification leads to **Centralization**.
- Conciliation leads to **Decentralization**.

- **STATUS RECIPROCITY**: Correlation between interstitial and intrinsic attributes, modulated by mixed synthesis.

| **STATUS RECIPROCITY** | **Dysfunctional Status** | **Functional Status** |
|----|---|---|
| **Interstitial Attributes** | Unification | Conciliation |
| **Intrinsic Attributes** | <plain>Centralization</plain> | <plain>Decentralization</plain> |

From here, a <plain>*centralized system*</plain> can be read as unifying underneath: lasting by gathering authority, even at the cost of function. A <plain>*decentralized system*</plain> can be read as conciliating: staying functional by rotating, splitting, or replacing its parts. The questions become usable:

- What are the highest dangers that block <plain>decentralization</plain> here?
- How could this centralized <plain>system</plain> become decentralized?
- What does each intrinsic status cost?
- Which frameworks would actually help a turn toward <plain>decentralization</plain>, rather than decorate it?

Some decentralized systems already try to live by iterated imperdurable <plain>functionality</plain>. **Blockchain implementations** — smart contracts, decentralized applications, DAOs — are designed around a <plain>decentralization</plain> philosophy that is uncommon in other digital systems. Their variety of frameworks makes them a live surface for <plain>Conciliatorics</plain>, not because the treatise is a blockchain treatise, but because the cut is already in the design.

The same cut belongs to government, and to local organizations that have to last without pretending a given <plain>arrangement</plain> is eternal. Design heuristics, tests of a turn, shared practices: all of that can be drawn from Status Reciprocity, and the drawing will, in return, pressure the theory to stay honest. The implementations are not a side hobby. They are how the interstitial names get tested against intrinsic ones.
