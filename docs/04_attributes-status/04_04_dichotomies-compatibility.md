# Dichotomies Compatibility

*On perpetual desire.*

Each longing is coherent in isolation. Together they are not. *Each longing predisposes a mixed <plain>system</plain> toward the undesirable pole of the other dichotomy*:

- **Functionality Longing predisposes Imperdurability**.
  - <plain>Symbolic representation: C≡L → (I≡ ^ I≢) = I≡.</plain>
  - While a mixed <plain>system</plain> is working, adversities — inner and outer — will test that working. If the <plain>arrangement</plain> cannot move, the condition is lost.

- **Perdurability Longing predisposes Dysfunctionality**.
  - <plain>Symbolic representation: I≢L → (C≡ ^ C≢) = C≢.</plain>
  - When a mixed <plain>system locks an arrangement</plain> so that it may last, and will not let it be revised, the locked form starts to fail. What then lasts is the failure.

This is the most contentious idea in <plain>Conciliatorics: the **incompatibility axiom**</plain>.

- **INCOMPATIBILITY AXIOM**: <plain>Functionality and Perdurability are **not compatible** in a mixed physiology system.</plain>

In theory that can sound modest. Humanity does not treat it as modest. We build as if a <plain>system</plain> could be functional in perpetuity, and we demand that the world conform:

- Nations and empires proclaim their *eternal inevitability*.
- Citizens vote for political parties that must remain in office for *the good of the people*.
- Family lineages pressure their members into *traditional obedience*.
- Religions promise eternal life in *heavenly bliss*.

Despite all of this, *empires fall, politics corrupts, families break, and Heaven is but a promise*.

Disagreement with the axiom is understandable — on philosophical grounds, or from attachment to the expectation of compatibility. Within <plain>Conciliatorics</plain> we treat it as true, because what follows is built on it. For the rest of this stretch, we continue as if it holds. The sequential <plain>conflict</plain> for each longing is then:

- **Functionality's tendency toward Imperdurability disrupts Perdurability**.
  - <plain>Symbolic representation: C≡ → I≢ = (C≡ ^ I≡).</plain>
  - Start with a perdurable <plain>system (I≢)</plain> and make it functional <plain>(C≡)</plain>, and you transform it into an imperdurable state <plain>(C≡ ^ I≡).</plain>

- **Perdurability's tendency toward Dysfunctionality disrupts Functionality**.
  - <plain>Symbolic representation: I≢ → C≡ = (C≢ ^ I≢).</plain>
  - Start with a functional <plain>system (C≡)</plain> and make it perdurable <plain>(I≢)</plain>, and you transform it into a dysfunctional state <plain>(C≢ ^ I≢).</plain>

That tension is the physiological status of every mixed interstitial <plain>system</plain>. Decay is how it falls.
