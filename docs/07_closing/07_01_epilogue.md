# Epilogue

*On goodbyes.*

To close, let's do a brief review of each chapter of this project.

## What this is

**Conciliatorics** is a perspective inside **Systemics**: it tries to reconcile the inventive side of intervening in systems. The starting model is **Behavior Dynamics** — one phenomenon (**Behavior**: members transforming a medium) and three entities (**System**, **Member**, **Medium**).

From there the treatise builds a four-parcel grid (method × focus):

| | Intrinsicality | Interstitiality |
|---|---|---|
| Contemplation | Sciences `(cS)` | Philosophy `)cS(` |
| Transformation | Technology `(tS)` | The Arts `)tS(` |

When edification turns inward and becomes an ideology, those become **ossifications** (Scientism, Technologism, Philosophism, Aestheticism). **Inventive Conciliation** restores function, via the **Parcels Council** — six standing articles, not a checklist.

The book then develops two parcels: **EIC** (Philosophy, Parts 2–5) in depth, and **EIT** (Technology, Part 6) as the concrete counterpart. Sciences and Arts are scoped out on purpose.

---

## Part 1 — Introduction

Sets the frame: Systemics as a container (not a forced unification of all systems work), Conciliatorics as the reconciling perspective inside it. Then:

- **Systemic Intervention** → **Inventive Intervention** (work the medium first)
- **Inventive Parcellation** — Contemplation/Transformation × Intrinsicality/Interstitiality
- **Edification** (continuous parcel development) vs **Ossification** (parcel over system; parentheses become brackets, `S` becomes `s`)
- **Conciliation** as ongoing restoration, never final

Philosophy is treated as the parcel most at risk of ossification, which is why EIC is developed first.

---

## Part 2 — Edified Interstitial Contemplation

EIC's model is **Attributes Dynamics**, bounded by two anchors:

- **Regulation** (opening rule set)
- **Synthesis** (closing integration)

Between them: **Structure** (interstitial anatomy) and **Status** (interstitial physiology).

Key commitments:

- Work with **attribute pairs**, not isolated attributes
- Five operators: `≡` symmetry, `≢` asymmetry, `^` concatenation, `←` accentuation, `→` modulation
- **Jurisdictions** with four boundary states: differentiation, overreach, dissociation, dissolution
- **Primary Jurisdiction**: Stillness/Movement (`Q`/`M`) and Simultaneity/Successiveness (`Si`/`Su`)
- Four **representation types**: discursive, symbolic, graphic, schematic

EIC stays on interstitial attributes on purpose. Unbounded philosophical scope is named as a main cause of philosophism.

---

## Part 3 — Attributes Structure (anatomy)

Accentuate the primary pairs → **Pro-Stillness** / **Pro-Movement** / **Pro-Simultaneity** / **Pro-Successiveness**. Concatenate those into four **Structure Quadrants**:

- Discernibility `D` = `pQ ^ pSu`
- Observability `O` = `pM ^ pSu`
- Resolutivity `R` = `pQ ^ pSi`
- Operativity `E` = `pM ^ pSi`

Accentuate a quadrant (keeping the other three in view) and you get **Interstitial Anatomy**: Discernment, Observation, Resolution, Operation (`iD`, `iO`, `iR`, `iE`). Potential state vs concrete action is kept distinct throughout.

Those four are then developed:

- **Discernment** — family tree down to six practical children: Property, Formation, Interaction, Preference, Standard, Criterion
- **Observation** — Assessment/Awareness → Acceptance; Relevance/Concentration → Alertness; both → Presence, treated as a resting point in a breathing cycle
- **Resolution** — Friction/Conflict × Chaos/Order → Potential, Stability, Suffering, Satisfaction → Temperance and Sacrifice → Viability
- **Operation** (originally the **Compass**) — Identity axis (Fronterization/Permeability) × Participation axis (Proceptivity/Receptivity) → Individual, Community, Orientation, Navigation

---

## Part 4 — Attributes Status (physiology)

Cross the primary pairs instead of accentuating inside them:

- Consistency `C` / Impermanence `I` (reinforcing)
- Balance `B` / Imbalance `b` (canceling; both sides must stay available)

That yields four **status dichotomies**: Functionality `C≡`, Dysfunctionality `C≢`, Imperdurability `I≡`, Perdurability `I≢`. Functionality and (counterintuitively) imperdurability are the desirable poles.

Physiology types:

- **Organic** — perdurability longing of living systems
- **Instrumental** — functionality longing of artifacts
- **Mixed** — both at once (humanity is the near example)

**Incompatibility Axiom**: in a mixed system, functionality and perdurability cannot coexist. Seeking both produces either imperdurable functionality or perdurable dysfunctionality.

**Status Decay**: sickness → death (organic); breakage → destruction (instrumental); crisis → dissolution (mixed). Only mixed systems keep a path beyond the end state.

---

## Part 5 — Attributes Synthesis

Physiology **modulates** anatomy (`oΣ`, `iΣ`, `mΣ`). Mixed synthesis splits:

- **Unification** `uΣ` — perdurable dysfunctionality (empire)
- **Conciliation** `cΣ` — imperdurable functionality (presidential term)

The **Conciliation Pathway** is iterated imperdurable functionality: like cells in a tissue — each instance dies; the larger function continues.

**Status Reciprocity** maps that onto familiar intrinsic terms: Unification ↔ Centralization, Conciliation ↔ Decentralization. Blockchain/DAOs are named as a live example of systems that already try to live this way.

Then the treatise draws a line:

- **Core EIC** — Attributes Dynamics through Status Reciprocity; complete and coherence-checked
- **Edge EIC** — further development of individual pieces, not required to be symmetric

**Operational Status** is the first Edge piece (author-marked draft): Sub/Supra (functional accentuation) and Hypo/Hyper (dysfunctional accentuation) applied to the four compass directions. Predator/prey and gut-barrier examples.

---

## Part 6 — Edified Intrinsic Transformation

EIT exists so EIC is not only tested against invented examples. The intended EIC/EIT relationship is **Systemic Diplomacy** (healthy differentiation, not overreach, silo, or amalgam).

Developed so far:

- **AI Assistance** — consistency checks, schematic case records, translation between representations, pattern search. Judgment (which pair a real case falls under) stays Contemplation; AI does not cross that line
- **YAML Library** — the schematic pattern, YAML chosen so both humans and AIs can read it natively
- **YAML Glossary** — first built instance (`library/glossary/glossary.yaml`); template for later instances (case library, diagram metadata, knowledge graph, operator grammar — those last ones are named, not built)

---

In closing, Conciliatorics is best seen not as a doctrine but as a toolkit for analysis, reflection, and participation. It encourages a shift in how we approach the systems we build, join, or inherit — and a continuous task of conciliation, because edification can ossify again at any moment. Whether applied to governance, technology, local organizations, or personal decision-making, the framework invites careful observation and thoughtful iteration.

This is only a starting point. The path forward depends on continued exploration, critique, and practical use. If this work helps clarify decisions, surface questions, or create space for deeper thinking, it has already served its purpose.
