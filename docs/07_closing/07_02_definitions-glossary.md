# Definitions Glossary

This is a curated list of core concepts, theoretical terms, and systemological language developed or redefined within *Conciliatorics*.

## Part 1: Introduction to Conciliatorics

- **META-DISCIPLINE**: A discipline (field of study) that deals with, or transcends, other disciplines.

- **SYSTEMICS**: A meta-discipline that groups different perspectives on the study of systems.

- **CONCILIATORICS**: A perspective within Systemics that seeks to reconcile the inventive aspect of Systemic Intervention.

---

- **BEHAVIOR DYNAMICS**: A model containing one main systemic phenomenon (**Behavior**) and three main systemic entities (**System**, **Member**, **Medium**).

- **BEHAVIOR**: The transformation of a medium by system members.

- **SYSTEM**: An environment where interrelated members express behavior.

- **MEMBER**: A unit that expresses behavior within a system.

- **MEDIUM**: Components of a system transformed by its members to express their behavior.

---

- **SYSTEMIC INTERVENTION**: Action performed by a member that seeks to influence the behavior of a system.

- **INTERVENER**: Member who intervenes in a system.

- **INVENTIVE SYSTEMIC INTERVENTION**: Exploration of a system's medium that seeks to influence its behavior.

---

- **INVENTIVE PARCELLATION**: Primary structure of inventive intervention.

- **INVENTIVE APPROACH**: Method of inventive intervention.

- **INVENTIVE POSITION**: Focus of inventive intervention.

- **CONTEMPLATION**: Inventive approach with emphasis on the *intervener*. Relates to *knowledge building* and *judgement*.

- **TRANSFORMATION**: Inventive approach with emphasis on the *system*. Relates to *concrete change* and *performance*.

- **INTRINSICALITY**: Inventive position with emphasis on the *intervenable*. Relates to *direct engagement* and *inherent properties*.

- **INTERSTITIALITY**: Inventive position with emphasis on the *systemic*. Relates to *intangible outcomes* and *relational properties*.

- **INVENTIVE PARCELS**: Main configurations of inventive parcellation: Intrinsic Contemplation, Intrinsic Transformation, Interstitial Contemplation and Interstitial Transformation.

---

- **INVENTIVE EDIFICATION**: Continuous and expansive development of inventive parcels.

- **EDIFIED INTRINSIC CONTEMPLATION**: Intrinsic Contemplation, edified. Field association — Sciences. Not developed in this treatise.

- **EDIFIED INTRINSIC TRANSFORMATION**: Intrinsic Transformation, edified. Field association — Technology. Developed in Part 6 as EIT.

- **EDIFIED INTERSTITIAL CONTEMPLATION**: Interstitial Contemplation, edified. Field association — Philosophy. Developed in depth as EIC (Parts 2-5).

- **EDIFIED INTERSTITIAL TRANSFORMATION**: Interstitial Transformation, edified. Field association — The Arts. Not developed in this treatise.

---

- **INVENTIVE OSSIFICATION**: The manifestation of dysfunctional processes in inventive edification.

- **OSSIFIED INTRINSIC CONTEMPLATION**: Ossified form of Edified Intrinsic Contemplation. Associated ideology — Scientism.

- **OSSIFIED INTRINSIC TRANSFORMATION**: Ossified form of Edified Intrinsic Transformation. Associated ideology — Technologism.

- **OSSIFIED INTERSTITIAL CONTEMPLATION**: Ossified form of Edified Interstitial Contemplation. Associated ideology — Philosophism.

- **OSSIFIED INTERSTITIAL TRANSFORMATION**: Ossified form of Edified Interstitial Transformation. Associated ideology — Aestheticism.

---

- **INVENTIVE CONCILIATION**: The restoration of functional processes in inventive edification.

- **PARCELS COUNCIL**: A structured method for facilitating conciliation.

---

## Part 2: Edified Interstitial Contemplation

- **ATTRIBUTE**: Specific quality of a system, member or a medium. In this framework, an attribute is not merely a passive trait but a dynamic component that interacts with behavior, structure, and purpose.

- **ATTRIBUTES DYNAMICS**: An EIC model designed to promote analytical exploration and integration of attributes.

- **ATTRIBUTES REGULATION**: EIC step that provides the rule set to explore the attributes structure and status.

- **ATTRIBUTES STRUCTURE**: EIC step that explores the interstitial anatomy components of Behavior Dynamics.

- **ATTRIBUTES STATUS**: EIC step that studies the interstitial physiology processes of Behavior Dynamics.

- **ATTRIBUTES SYNTHESIS**: EIC step that integrates the exploration of attributes structure and status.

---

- **ATTRIBUTE POSITION**: Focus of systemic attributes. The two main attributes positions are *intrinsic attributes* and *interstitial attributes*.

- **ATTRIBUTE DISPOSITION**: The resulting outcome of the entire EIC journey. Only its trajectory is stated at introduction; fully resolved at the close of Part 5 (see Status Reciprocity).

- **ATTRIBUTE PAIR**: The fundamental systemic unit in EIC, used instead of isolated attributes. Inherently contains Side A, Side B, and the dynamic relationship between them — held in *symmetry* if both sides carry equal importance, *asymmetry* if one is prioritized.

- **SYMMETRY**: `≡` — balanced, mutually-interdependent relationship between the sides of an attribute pair.

- **ASYMMETRY**: `≢` — directional prioritization within an attribute pair; broken symmetry.

- **CONCATENATION**: `^` — interaction between different attribute pairs, or between attributes of different paired sets.

- **ACCENTUATION**: `←` — focus on one attribute of a paired set, or on one element of a compounded interaction, without dismissing the other side.

- **MODULATION**: `→` — the concrete effect of one attribute element or compound over another; alters internal dynamics rather than heightening or combining.

- **SYSTEMIC JURISDICTION**: The bounded environment that contains Conciliatorics components.

- **ATTRIBUTE JURISDICTION**: The bounded environment that contains Attributes Dynamics components.

- **JURISDICTIONAL OVERREACH**: The process by which one jurisdiction breaks boundaries to exert control over others.

- **JURISDICTIONAL DIFFERENTIATION**: The process and systemic state in which boundaries are maintained. The jurisdiction is aware of its distinct role while cultivating healthy interactions—this promotes **systemic diplomacy**.

- **JURISDICTIONAL DISSOCIATION**: The process and systemic state in which boundaries fracture. The jurisdiction isolates itself, forming a **systemic silo**.

- **JURISDICTIONAL DISSOLUTION**: The process and systemic state in which boundaries merge. The jurisdiction fuses into others, forming a **systemic amalgam**.

- **PRIMARY JURISDICTION**: The initial environment of attribute pairs within Attributes Dynamics.

- **STILLNESS**: `Q` — primary attribute; spatial pole of rest.

- **MOVEMENT**: `M` — primary attribute; spatial pole of motion.

- **SIMULTANEITY**: `Si` — primary attribute; temporal pole of co-occurrence.

- **SUCCESSIVENESS**: `Su` — primary attribute; temporal pole of sequence.

---

- **DISCURSIVE REPRESENTATION**: Representation through written or spoken language. The traditional medium of philosophical discourse, carrying argument, nuance, and context.

- **SYMBOLIC REPRESENTATION**: Representation through compact notation—operators, letters, and equations—that holds dense relationships in a single, precise, reusable form.

- **GRAPHIC REPRESENTATION**: Representation through diagrams and images, exploiting spatial and visual relationships to make a structure legible at a glance.

- **SCHEMATIC REPRESENTATION**: Representation through structured, field-based records. Its principal value is storage: material that can be filled in, compared, and accumulated into a library without collapsing into disorder as it grows.

## Part 3: Attributes Structure

- **ATTRIBUTE ACCENTUATION**: Temporary focus on one member of an attribute pair.

- **STRUCTURE QUADRANT**: Concatenation of two attribute accentuations, creating a potential interstitial state.

- **INTERSTITIAL ANATOMY**: Set of concrete interstitial actions, composed of structure quadrants accentuations.

| **ANATOMY TERMS** | **Potential State** | **Concrete Action** | **General Context Term** |
|---|---|---|---|
| **To Discern** | Discernibility | Discernment | Interstitial Discernment |
| **To Observe** | Observability | Observation | Interstitial Observation  |
| **To Resolve** | Resolutivity | Resolution | Interstitial Resolution  |
| **To Operate** | Operativity | Operation | Interstitial Operation  |

---

- **BOUNDED DISCERNMENT/OBSERVATION**: Quadrant depending on member's perspective.

- **NON-BOUNDED DISCERNMENT/OBSERVATION**: Quadrant detached from member's presence.

- **OBJECTIVITY**: Non-bounded discernment.

- **SUBJECTIVITY**: Bounded discernment.

- **ASSERTION**: Assertive discernment—discernment that states facts and perspectives about the world.

- **ARRANGEMENT**: Arranging discernment—discernment that organizes aspects of a phenomenon to enhance its comprehension.

- **DISCERNMENT ASCENDANCY**: One of four aspects—Veracity, Congruence, Appreciation, Evaluation—formed by intersecting objectivity/subjectivity with assertion/arrangement, each heavily influencing how discernment operates.

- **VERACITY**: Discernment ascendancy formed by Objective Assertion.

- **CONGRUENCE**: Discernment ascendancy formed by Objective Arrangement.

- **APPRECIATION**: Discernment ascendancy formed by Subjective Assertion.

- **EVALUATION**: Discernment ascendancy formed by Subjective Arrangement.

- **DISCERNMENT CHILDREN**: The practical terms produced by the authority balance between discernment parents (Veracity, Congruence, Appreciation, Evaluation): Property, Formation, Interaction, Preference, Standard, and Criterion.

- **PROPERTY**: Discernment child formed when Veracity has authority over Congruence.

- **FORMATION**: Discernment child formed when Congruence has authority over Veracity.

- **INTERACTION**: Discernment child formed when Veracity and Congruence hold equal authority.

- **PREFERENCE**: Discernment child formed when Appreciation has authority over Evaluation.

- **STANDARD**: Discernment child formed when Evaluation has authority over Appreciation.

- **CRITERION**: Discernment child formed when Appreciation and Evaluation hold equal authority.

---

- **JUDGEMENT**: Bounded observation—attaching one's own perspectives, priorities, and biases to the act of observing.

- **ATTENTION**: Non-bounded observation—attending to the world without the observer's own judgement.

- **OBSERVATION FORMS**: The four concrete forms produced by combining judgement/attention with a top-down or bottom-up perspective: Assessment, Awareness, Relevance, and Concentration.

- **ASSESSMENT**: Observation form combining a top-down perspective with judgement—placing oneself as judge of the world.

- **AWARENESS**: Observation form combining a top-down perspective with attention—contemplating the landscape without judgement.

- **RELEVANCE**: Observation form combining a bottom-up perspective with judgement—assigning a priority order to the world's manifestations.

- **CONCENTRATION**: Observation form combining a bottom-up perspective with attention—focusing on one aspect of the world outside immediate judgement.

- **ACCEPTANCE**: Integration of Assessment and Awareness; the state of holistic attention and appropriate judgement.

- **ALERTNESS**: Integration of Relevance and Concentration; the state of open concentration and available relevance.

- **PRESENCE**: Integration of Acceptance and Alertness; the state of fullness observation. Treated as a resting point to return to, not a final destination.

---

- **CHAOS / ORDER**: Main duality of resolution challenges in complex systems; neither implies wrongness nor rightness on its own.

- **FRICTION / CONFLICT**: Second challenge duality of resolution—friction relates to structural challenges, conflict to behavioral challenges.

- **POTENTIAL**: Friction concatenated with Chaos.

- **STABILITY**: Friction concatenated with Order.

- **SUFFERING**: Conflict concatenated with Chaos.

- **SATISFACTION**: Conflict concatenated with Order.

- **TEMPERANCE**: Integration of Potential and Stability; forward-facing, dynamic regulation that allows exploration without losing one's foundations.

- **SACRIFICE**: Integration of Suffering and Satisfaction; temporally-aware, delayed gratification—accepting present suffering to enjoy future satisfaction.

- **VIABILITY**: Integration of Temperance and Sacrifice; the reconciliation of one's interests, priorities, and desires with the limitations of the world.

---

- **IDENTITY AXIS**: Horizontal axis of the Operation compass; the concatenation of Fronterization and Permeability.

- **PARTICIPATION AXIS**: Vertical axis of the Operation compass; the concatenation of Proceptivity and Receptivity.

- **PROCEPTIVITY**: Cardinal North of the Operation compass; active engagement in systemic participation.

- **RECEPTIVITY**: Cardinal South of the Operation compass; openness to one's environment, calibrating participation.

- **FRONTERIZATION**: Cardinal East of the Operation compass; the boundaries that give shape to identity.

- **PERMEABILITY**: Cardinal West of the Operation compass; the capacity of identity to adapt to the world's flow.

- **INDIVIDUAL**: North-East hemisphere of the Operation compass; Proceptivity concatenated with Fronterization.

- **COMMUNITY**: South-West hemisphere of the Operation compass; Receptivity concatenated with Permeability.

- **ORIENTATION**: South-East hemisphere of the Operation compass; Receptivity concatenated with Fronterization.

- **NAVIGATION**: North-West hemisphere of the Operation compass; Proceptivity concatenated with Permeability.

- **COMPASS**: The original, informal name for Operation—still used, especially alongside the diagram, but not interchangeable with "Operation" when referring to the full Interstitial Anatomy context (i.e., alongside Discernment, Observation, and Resolution).

## Part 4: Attributes Status

- **ATTRIBUTE CONCATENATION**: Crossed interaction between attribute pairs from the primary jurisdiction.

- **CONSISTENCY / IMPERMANENCE**: Reinforcing attribute pair produced by attribute concatenation — Consistency is Stillness concatenated with Simultaneity; Impermanence is Movement concatenated with Successiveness. More unstable than primary-jurisdiction pairs: any accentuation carries a greater risk of breaking the pair.

- **BALANCE / IMBALANCE**: Canceling attribute pair produced by attribute concatenation — Balance is Stillness concatenated with Successiveness; Imbalance is Movement concatenated with Simultaneity. Indivisible: both sides must remain available. Balance does not imply superiority over Imbalance.

- **STATUS DICHOTOMIES**: Concatenation between reinforcing and canceling attribute pairs.

- **FUNCTIONALITY**: Consistency accentuated over Impermanence, under a symmetrical (available) Balance/Imbalance status. Desirable.

- **DYSFUNCTIONALITY**: Consistency accentuated over Impermanence, under an asymmetrical (detrimental) status. Harmful.

- **IMPERDURABILITY**: Impermanence accentuated over Consistency, under a symmetrical status. Counterintuitively desirable.

- **PERDURABILITY**: Impermanence accentuated over Consistency, under an asymmetrical status. Counterintuitively detrimental.

- **INTERSTITIAL LONGING**: Survival and performance drive.

- **PERDURABILITY LONGING**: The survival drive of biological systems; interacts with the Imperdurability/Perdurability dichotomy to form Organic Physiology.

- **FUNCTIONALITY LONGING**: The performance ambition of artificial systems; interacts with the Functional/Dysfunctional dichotomy to form Instrumental Physiology.

- **INTERSTITIAL PHYSIOLOGY**: Set of interactions between interstitial longings and status dichotomies.

---

- **ORGANIC PHYSIOLOGY**: Interaction between "perdurability longing" and "imperdurability / perdurability dichotomy".

- **INSTRUMENTAL PHYSIOLOGY**: Interaction between "functionality longing" and "functional / dysfunctional dichotomy".

- **MIXED PHYSIOLOGY**: Simultaneous interaction between both interstitial longings and status dichotomies.

---

- **INCOMPATIBILITY AXIOM**: Functionality and Perdurability are not compatible in a mixed physiology system.

- **STATUS DECAY**: Interstitial process of physiological deterioration.

## Part 5: Attributes Synthesis

- **ORGANIC SYNTHESIS**: Modulation of interstitial anatomy by organic physiology. 

- **INSTRUMENTAL SYNTHESIS**: Modulation of interstitial anatomy by instrumental physiology. 

- **MIXED SYNTHESIS**: Modulation of interstitial anatomy by mixed physiology. 

---

- **UNIFICATION SYNTHESIS**: Modulation of interstitial anatomy by perdurable dysfunctionality physiology. 

- **CONCILIATION SYNTHESIS**: Modulation of interstitial anatomy by imperdurable functionality physiology. 

- **CONCILIATION PATHWAY**: Iterated Imperdurable Functionality.

- **STATUS RECIPROCITY**: Correlation between attribute position and attribute disposition, influenced by mixed synthesis.

- **CENTRALIZATION**: Intrinsic-attribute correlate of Unification Synthesis; the dysfunctional-status pole of Status Reciprocity.

- **DECENTRALIZATION**: Intrinsic-attribute correlate of Conciliation Synthesis; the functional-status pole of Status Reciprocity.

---

- **CORE EIC**: The foundational, integrated development of EIC, running from Attributes Dynamics through the close of Attributes Synthesis.

- **EDGE EIC**: Additional EIC development extending beyond Core EIC into specific, still-evolving methods. Individual Edge EIC concepts don't need to cover every Core EIC component symmetrically, and can grow independently of one another.

- **OPERATIONAL STATUS**: The first developed Edge EIC concept — functional/dysfunctional gradation, via Sub/Supra/Hypo/Hyper modulators, applied to the Operation compass's four cardinal directions. Currently developed for Operation only; source prose is an author-marked draft.

- **SUB / SUPRA**: Functional accentuation modulators — reduced (Sub) or heightened (Supra) emphasis on an Operation direction — that preserve complementarity with its pair, paralleling Symmetry.

- **HYPO / HYPER**: Dysfunctional accentuation modulators — reduced (Hypo) or heightened (Hyper) emphasis on an Operation direction — whose emphasis has broken complementarity with its pair, paralleling Asymmetry. Not an "Anti-" framing: they mark a dysfunctional degree of the same direction, not opposition to it.

## Part 6: Edified Intrinsic Transformation

- **SYSTEMIC DIPLOMACY**: A jurisdiction's awareness of its own distinct role, cultivating healthy interaction with neighboring jurisdictions rather than isolating itself (dissociation) or merging into them (dissolution).

- **AI ASSISTANCE**: The use of AI systems to support work with Conciliatorics — maintaining terminological and notational consistency, structuring applied case readings, translating a concept or case between representation types, and traversing an accumulated case library for patterns across cases. Interpretive judgement — deciding which jurisdiction, pair, and attributes a real case falls under — remains Contemplation, and is not something AI assistance performs or automates away.

- **YAML LIBRARY**: A structured, growing set of schematic entries—sharing a consistent set of fields and cross-referenced by stable identifiers—that accumulates instances of some kind (definitions, symbols, cases, relationships) into something that stays organized as it grows.

- **YAML GLOSSARY**: The first built instance of a YAML Library; holds every defined term and symbol in the treatise as a single set of structured, cross-referenced records, replacing the separate definitions and symbols glossaries.
