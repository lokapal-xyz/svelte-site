# Systemic Diplomacy

*On good neighbors.*

Everything developed so far belongs to one Inventive Parcel: Edified Interstitial Contemplation. That narrowing was deliberate — Attribute Position already said so. EIC works interstitial attributes, the qualities that hold between things, not the inherent properties of any particular thing. That is a sound way to build a theory. It is not, on its own, a sound way to test one. A framework that only ever proves itself against invented examples risks becoming a fantasy dressed as rigor: precise, internally coherent, and untouched by the <plain>friction</plain> of anything real. The remedy is not to abandon EIC's discipline. It is to give the treatise a second parcel whose job is exactly the <plain>friction</plain> EIC was built to set aside.

Part 1 already named where that parcel has to live. Of the four Inventive Parcels, only one shares no axis with EIC: **Edified Intrinsic Transformation**, associated with Technology. Where EIC studies how attributes relate in the abstract, Intrinsic Transformation engages the inherent properties of specific, concrete things — this file format rather than that one, this country's economy rather than that one — and *does* something with them. Building a schema, choosing a serialization, encoding a real case: all of it sits on the intrinsic side of the position axis, in the concrete-change territory of Transformation. EIC was never going to do this work. It set the ground and moved on.

- **EDIFIED INTRINSIC TRANSFORMATION**: Transformation × Intrinsicality, edified. Field association — Technology. Developed here as EIT.

We'll use **EIT** as the acronym from here. EIC and EIT share a letter that means two different things — Interstitial in one, Intrinsic in the other — and a careless reading could carry the first meaning into the second. No new convention is required. Spelling the term out on first use in a chapter, as here, is enough. `EIC` keeps its established form rather than being retrofitted, the same way **Compass** survived beside Operation. The remaining two parcels — Edified Intrinsic Contemplation and Edified Interstitial Transformation, the Sciences and the Arts — are not being developed, and are not given working acronyms. That is a scoping decision: this treatise's implementation work runs through Technology, not through a survey of all four fields at once.

The relationship between the two developed parcels already has a name.

- **SYSTEMIC DIPLOMACY**: A jurisdiction aware of its distinct role, cultivating healthy <plain>interaction</plain> with neighboring jurisdictions rather than isolating itself or merging into them.

It was introduced in Attribute Jurisdiction as what Jurisdictional Differentiation promotes. EIC and EIT are now two adjacent jurisdictions, and their relationship is the first real test of that concept rather than a description of it. EIC supplies the relational structure — the equations, the operators, the account of a functional or dysfunctional interstitial state. EIT supplies engagement with something concrete enough to apply that structure to. Neither replaces the other. The boundary has to stay visible:

- **Overreach** would look like EIC's theory being quietly revised to suit an implementation's convenience, or EIT's tooling dictating what a term is allowed to mean.
- **Dissociation** would look like EIT drifting far enough from EIC's vocabulary that it stops being a Conciliatorics implementation and becomes a schema tool that happens to share a glossary.
- **Dissolution** would look like the two blurring until a reader can no longer tell which claims are theoretical and which are implementation choices made for convenience.

Engagement without either side losing its distinct role: that is Systemic Diplomacy, rather than standoff or merger. What follows is EIT's first developed content — not a survey of everything Intrinsic Transformation could be, but a starting model, as Attributes Dynamics was EIC's starting model rather than the whole of Interstitial Contemplation.
