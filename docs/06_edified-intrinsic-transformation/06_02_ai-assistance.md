# AI Assistance

*On new interveners.*

The clearest place to start EIT is with tools already doing intrinsic, concrete work on this material: AI systems that can read, organize, and cross-check dense, symbol-laden, self-referential text. What follows is an account of what that assistance can realistically do today. It is kept modest on purpose. Overstating it here would undercut the discipline EIT exists to bring.

- **AI ASSISTANCE**: The use of AI systems to support work with <plain>Conciliatorics</plain> — maintaining <plain>consistency</plain>, traversing accumulated records for patterns, and setting a schematic record forth in the other representation types.

Schematic Representation is the natural interface. Discursive prose has to be parsed for meaning, symbolic notation interpreted against a key, a diagram visually recognized. A schematic record, built from explicit fields and stated relationships, can be read and checked the way a table is read rather than a paragraph decoded. That is why <plain>AI assistance and Schematic Representation</plain> belong in the same jurisdiction: the representation type an AI can hold most accurately is also the one best suited to being acted on reliably.

In practice:

- **Maintaining <plain>consistency</plain>.** As terms, symbols, and jurisdictions accumulate, an AI can check that a new symbol does not collide with an existing one, that a term's use matches its definition, and that cross-references stay accurate — exhaustive work for a human to sustain by hand, and straightforward for an AI without fatigue. This is not hypothetical. The Synthesis symbols originally read `oS` / `iS` / `mS` / `uS` / `cS`, and `cS` collided with an unrelated symbol from Inventive Edification. That collision was caught this way and resolved by moving the whole set to `Σ`.

- **Case records.** When a real case is being analyzed — a policy, an organization, a historical episode — the analyst decides which jurisdiction, pair, and attributes apply, and writes that placement into the record. A useful discipline, still informal, is treating certain pairs as mandatory: if a case is read through Fronterization and Permeability, a record with only one side filled in should read as incomplete, not as a finished analysis that happened to focus on one attribute. Enforcing that completeness is exactly the kind of check a schema — and an AI checking against it — can hold a human analyst to.

- **From the record outward.** A case or concept held schematically can be turned into discursive prose or symbolic notation on request. The compact record stays the source of truth; a full written account remains available whenever one is wanted. The account does not run back into the record.

- **Traversing accumulated material.** Once enough case records exist, an AI can search across them for patterns a person reading case by case would be unlikely to notice — every recorded instance, say, of an Identity Axis asymmetry that preceded a Unification-type outcome, across otherwise unrelated cases.

None of this is the interpretive step. Deciding that a given policy is a matter of <plain>Permeability and Fronterization</plain> at all, and characterizing where it sits on each, is Contemplation: <plain>judgement</plain> applied to a specific, messy, real situation. An AI can enforce that a reading is complete once it exists. It cannot supply the reading. That is not a gap to be engineered away. It follows from <plain>Contemplation</plain> as the approach concerned with the <plain>intervener's knowledge-building and judgement — a role Transformation</plain> was never meant to take over.

One further extension is worth naming as a direction and nothing more: a small derivation engine — a program that takes a *completed* schematic reading and mechanically checks or derives what the Attributes Status equations say should follow from it — may eventually be possible. It would still only run on <plain>judgement</plain> a human had already supplied. It would not perform the categorization. No such engine exists yet, and nothing here claims that one is imminent. It is on the record as a possible future direction for EIT, alongside whatever other applications its readers bring to the parcel. Which of these uses an AI may perform, and which remain the intervener's, is a matter of direction. Schematic Vectors, later in this part, names the directions.
