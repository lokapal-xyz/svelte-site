## They say

Plato's *Republic* designs a city that can last because the wisest rule, the guardians are educated not to treat office as a prize, and the noble lie binds the classes to their work. The fear underneath is drift: if the form changes, justice collapses. Philosophy, in that picture, is not one parcel among others. It is the upper bound of the city.

A reader who knows even the cartoon of the philosopher-king already has the hope: put knowledge in charge, and the arrangement need not be revised.

## Conciliatorics' view

Philosophism is interstitial contemplation ossified: the parcel that demands that life pass through it. Unification Synthesis lasts by gathering authority, even at the cost of function. A kallipolis that must not change is, in these names, both — a contemplative elite as filter, and a form that treats its own perdurability as the good.

EIC's field association already is Philosophy. Attribute Position narrowed the job: interstitial attributes, not a theory of the ceramic, and not a license to rule the other parcels. Inventive Conciliation is standing, not a once-for-all founding.

## Where they meet

Both take seriously that a city can be wrecked by office-seekers and by unexamined appetites. Design matters. Education matters. A form that is only a bargain among factions is not yet justice, and not yet function.

## Where they part

Plato wants a lasting order under philosophy. Conciliatorics treats that want as the mixed system's perdurability longing, and as a main road into philosophism. Guardians who must not be replaced, a lie that must not be unmasked, a city that must not iterate: those are unification wearing a classical dress. The handshake is the seriousness. The jobs remain different. The *Republic* is not a failed EIC; EIC is not a repaired *Republic*.
