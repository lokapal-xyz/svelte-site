## They say

Antonio Gramsci's prison notebooks made hegemony portable: domination that works through consent, so that one group's outlook feels like common sense. Civil society, organic intellectuals, a war of position — the furniture travelled far beyond the Italian case. A reader who has only "hegemony" already has a filter that does not look like a filter.

## Conciliatorics' view

Inventive Ossification is a parcel that demands to be the filter for Behavior Dynamics. Scientism, Technologism, Philosophism, Aestheticism are named ideologies of that demand. Gramsci's hegemony is nearby as a cultural-political fact: a common sense that is not common.

EIC does not take up class, the party, or the war of position. The contact is the filter that feels like the world, not a theory of revolution.

## Where they meet

Marx's ideology, Weber's cage, Habermas's colonization, and Gramsci's hegemony rhyme as warnings about a made outlook that runs the life. A reader expects this plate next to Marx.

## Where they part

Gramsci is writing a Marxist theory of politics and culture, in prison, toward a different hegemony. Conciliatorics is not that project, and it will not call conciliation a counter-hegemony. Common sense is not an operator. The handshake is the filter. The jobs remain different.
