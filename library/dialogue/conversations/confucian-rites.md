## They say

The *Analects* treat names and rites as how a people holds together. The line most readers will have heard, if they have heard one, is the rectification of names (*zhengming*): if names are not correct, speech will not be in accordance with things, and affairs will not succeed (*Analects* 13.3). Another is the pairing of ruler and ruled, father and son, each in its name (12.11). *Li* — rites, ritual propriety, the forms of living together — is not ornament. It is the practiced shape of those names.

On that picture, disorder is not first a shortage of force. It is names that no longer match what people do, and rites that have gone hollow.

## Conciliatorics' view

A custom that cannot be revised is, in this framework, on the way to ossification: an edification that has begun to demand that life pass through it. Inventive Ossification is that reversal across four parcels. A family that treats a custom as unamendable, versus one that lets a generation revise the custom and remain a family, is the same dilemma Mixed Synthesis names at a smaller scale.

Discernment has a child for the shared "this is what counts as a mug here": a standard. Names that hold a practice are not nothing. They are how members arrange a phenomenon so that it can be grasped together. The question is whether the name is still serving Behavior Dynamics, or whether Behavior Dynamics has been asked to serve the name.

## Where they meet

Both take names and practiced forms as load-bearing, not as labels stuck on afterwards. A polity that no longer means what it says, a rite that is only costume, a title that does not bind: those are already failures of holding-together. A reader who has used the *Analects* to say why "just be authentic" is not a politics will hear the weight.

## Where they part

Confucian repair often looks like restoring a proper, lasting <plain>order</plain>: rectify the names, return the rites to life. Conciliatorics can grant the diagnosis and still refuse the lasting as the good. An unamendable *li* is unification wearing a classical dress. Conciliation would be the generation that revises the custom and remains a family — names still in use, and not a cage. *Ren*, filiality, and the rest of the Confucian vocabulary are not imported. The handshake is real. The jobs remain different.
