## They say

Aldo Leopold's land ethic, in *A Sand County Almanac* (1949), enlarges the <plain>community</plain> to soils, waters, plants, and animals. A thing is right when it tends to preserve the integrity, stability, and beauty of the biotic <plain>community</plain>. A reader who has only that sentence already has an interstitial move: the quality holds between members of a land, not only in a human will.

## Conciliatorics' view

Attribute Position keeps EIC on interstitial attributes — qualities that hold between, not the ceramic as such. Leopold's enlargement is nearby as an ethical fact: the land as relation. It is not EIC's job to become a land ethic. The sciences and the arts have work here that Philosophy, in this framework, should not absorb.

Integrity and stability, in Leopold's sentence, lean toward a lasting biotic whole. Conciliatorics will hear perdurability longing in "stability," and still grant that a relation to land is not a private taste.

## Where they meet

Both refuse a world of objects that have no between. Daoist pairing, Kant's plate on this site, Holling's landscape, and Leopold's land can sit with Attribute Position as a family of "the between is real."

## Where they part

Leopold is writing an ethic of conservation, with a beauty that is his. Conciliatorics is not that ethic. Biotic community is not Community in Operation's sense — another homonym. Stability is not the desirable pole here. The handshake is the relation. The jobs remain different. Ecology's second plate is a contact, not a conversion.
