## They say

F. A. Hayek's "The Use of Knowledge in Society" (1945) is the short essay most readers will know if they know him for one economic claim. The knowledge needed to coordinate a society is not given to any one mind. It is dispersed, local, often tacit, and changing. A center that tries to gather it all, and then issue a plan, is not merely ambitious. It is asking for a kind of knowledge that does not exist in one place. Prices, on Hayek's account, are a way that scattered knowledge can be used without being collected.

The political afterlife of that essay is larger than the essay. The contact here is the epistemic one.

## Conciliatorics' view

Status Reciprocity reads an interstitial choice in ordinary names. Unification leads to centralization: lasting by gathering authority, even at the cost of function. Conciliation leads to decentralization: staying functional by rotating, splitting, or replacing parts. If no center can hold the knowledge a mixed case needs, that is one reason gathering authority so that the form may last is a functional cost, not only a moral one.

The questions that follow are the same ones Status Reciprocity already asks. What blocks decentralization here? What does each intrinsic status cost? Which frameworks would actually help a turn, rather than decorate it?

## Where they meet

Both refuse the picture of a mind at the center that already knows enough. Dispersed knowledge, local craft, a price or a rule that communicates without collecting: a reader who has used Hayek to doubt the omniscient planner will recognize why Conciliatorics treats centralization as unification underneath. The gathering is the point, and the gathering is the problem.

## Where they part

Hayek's scene is markets, prices, and the critique of central planning. Conciliatorics is not a market theory, and Status Reciprocity is not a vote for laissez-faire. Families, churches, protocols, and polities are live surfaces for the same cut. A term from economics is welcome when it does not hinder the work. It is not rewritten until EIC has a price theory. The handshake is the limit of the center. The jobs remain different.
