## They say

In *The German Ideology* and the 1859 Preface, Marx and Engels treated ruling ideas as the ideas of the ruling class, and the economic structure as the real ground on which a legal and political superstructure rises. Ideology, in the critical use, is a reversal: thought presenting itself as the base of life, while life is already organized elsewhere. The point is not that ideas do not matter. It is that they can wear a false independence.

A reader who has only "base and superstructure" already has a warning about parcels that claim to be the ground.

## Conciliatorics' view

Inventive Parcellation splits contemplation from transformation, intrinsicality from interstitiality. The four edifications — sciences, technology, philosophy, the arts, as field associations — are meant to serve Behavior Dynamics, not the other way around. Ossification is the reversal: the edification becomes an ideology (Scientism, Technologism, Philosophism, Aestheticism) and demands that members, medium, and <plain>system</plain> pass through its filter.

That is a cousin of Marx's reversal, named across four parcels rather than as a theory of class society. EIC does not take up surplus value, and it does not rewrite historical materialism as Attributes Dynamics.

## Where they meet

Both describe a tool of thought that starts to run the life it was supposed to clarify. Both refuse the story that the most articulate doctrine is therefore the ground. A scientific <plain>community</plain> that will only admit other parcels in scientific terms is already in the neighborhood, whether or not one uses the word ideology.

## Where they part

Marx's scene is political economy, class, and emancipation. Conciliatorics' scene is inventive parcels inside Systemics. Class is not a Dex id. The Incompatibility Axiom is not a mode of production. Importing "base" as if it were Behavior Dynamics would dissolve a jurisdiction EIC is trying to keep. The handshake is the reversal. The jobs remain different.
