## They say

Western philosophy was, for a long stretch, the upper bound of knowledge. What we now call the sciences lived inside it. Once the scientific method emancipated those inquiries and took a predominant place in culture, philosophy had to say what it still was. One durable answer has been metaphysics: an account of what exists in Reality, of the conditions under which anything can appear as an object at all.

Kant is the face of that answer most readers will know without having lived in the first *Critique*. Space and time, in that book, are not more furniture in the world. They are forms of intuition — the conditions under which anything is given to us. The ceramic mug is encountered in space; the hour it is used is encountered in time. The philosopher's job, on this picture, is not to compete with the chemist about the glaze. It is to say how such an encounter is possible.

## Conciliatorics' view

EIC's field association already is Philosophy. Attribute Position does not try to win that field back from the sciences. It narrows the job. The ceramic and the glass are intrinsic: they belong to the materials, and to the parcels that work inherent properties. That a mug is *yours* is interstitial. It arises from a relation, and it is not inherent to you or to the mug.

EIC stays with interstitial attributes. Primary Jurisdiction begins in spatial and temporal pairs — Stillness and Movement, Simultaneity and Successiveness — because a garden is already plots and paths, and the hour they are in. Those pairs are a root we can walk back to. They are not a claim about the structure of Reality. A theory of ceramic that never speaks to the potter is free to be ingenious. It is not free to call that ingenuity a contribution to the other parcels.

## Where they meet

Both refuse to treat space and time as just two more objects on the shelf. How things show up — at rest or in motion, in sequence or together — is not a side issue. A reader who has used Kant as a doorway into "what philosophy is for, after physics" will hear an echo in Attribute Position's cut.

## Where they part

Kant is still giving the conditions of possible experience of objects. EIC does not claim that jurisdiction. It does not become more ambitious by taking the ceramic back. It becomes more useful by staying with what is interstitial, and by remaining able to hand the ceramic to the sciences, technology, and the arts. Unregulated philosophical scope — the license to analyze everything that feels appropriate — is, from this point of view, one of the main causes of philosophism. The positioning is a discipline, not a lack of nerve.
