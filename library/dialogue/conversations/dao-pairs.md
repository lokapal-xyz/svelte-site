## They say

The *Daodejing* and the *Zhuangzi* treat opposites as generating rather than as a contest to be won: yin and yang, hard and soft, useful and empty. Wuwei — non-forcing, acting without strain against the grain — is the portable political and ethical claim. A reader who has Confucian rites on this site already expects a Daoist plate as the other classical Chinese contact: pairing and non-forcing, not rectification of names.

## Conciliatorics' view

An attribute pair is both sides and the relation, or it is not the unit. Accentuation focuses without dismissing. Operation prefers proceptive and receptive to masculine and feminine, and still grants that those older names are valid. Wuwei is nearby as receptivity: accurate engagement has to receive before it pushes. It is not the whole compass.

EIC does not take up the Dao as a metaphysics, and it does not rewrite wuwei as an operator. Positioning here means a contact, not a commentary.

## Where they meet

Both refuse a contest in which one side should disappear. Hegel's remaining-versus-sublation, Berlin's unfused liberties, Confucian names, and Daoist pairing can sit with the attribute pair. A buffet of this kind without yin-yang would be noticed.

## Where they part

Daoist teaching is a way, a politics of non-forcing, a set of stories. Conciliatorics is not that way. Yin-yang is not Symmetry. Wuwei is not Receptivity, even when they rhyme. The handshake is the generating two, and the non-forced act. The jobs remain different. Do not import the *Daodejing* as Attributes Regulation.
