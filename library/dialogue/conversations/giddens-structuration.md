## They say

Anthony Giddens's *Constitution of Society* (1984) refused to start with structure or with agency. Structuration is the duality: structures are both the medium and the outcome of practices, reproduced in action. A reader who has only "structure and agency" already has the refusal of a first entity.

## Conciliatorics' view

Behavior Dynamics treats Behavior, System, Member, and Medium as simultaneous. You do not first have a <plain>system</plain> and then add members, or first have a medium and then wait for behavior. The <plain>arrangement</plain> is an intellectual device, not a claim about which one is more real. Giddens is nearby as a sociological fact: no queue.

EIC does not take up Giddens's rules and resources as structure, and it does not rewrite structuration as an operator. The contact is the simultaneity.

## Where they meet

Both refuse a building-block story that puts one piece down first. GST's open systems, Meadows' feedback, and Giddens's duality can sit with Behavior Dynamics as a family of "not a queue." A reader of social theory expects this plate.

## Where they part

Giddens is writing a general social theory, with modernity as a later theme. Conciliatorics is not that theory. Structure is not System. Agency is not Member. Importing structuration as Behavior Dynamics would be a second starting model pretending to be the first. Other starting models can be compatible. They are not required to dissolve into this one.
