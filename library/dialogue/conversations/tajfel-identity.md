## They say

Henri Tajfel's minimal-group experiments showed how little it takes to make an in-group: a trivial category, then favoritism. With John Turner, social identity theory made "us and them" a normal operation of self, not a pathology reserved for extremists. A reader who has only "in-group bias" already has the cut. Schmitt and Putnam belong at the edges of this plate.

## Conciliatorics' view

Operation's identity axis is permeability and fronterization. Us-and-them is nearby as a psychological fact of fronterization. The other side of the axis has to remain available: a chamber that cannot open is a tomb. Social identity is not required to become an enemy. The pair is the point.

EIC does not take up Tajfel's matrices, and it does not rewrite social identity as an operator. The contact is the easy border, and the duty not to let it be the whole.

## Where they meet

Schmitt's friend-enemy, Putnam's practiced many, Hirschman's stay-or-go, and Tajfel's minimal groups can sit with the identity axis. A reader of social psychology expects this plate.

## Where they part

Tajfel is writing an experimental social psychology of intergroup behavior. Conciliatorics is not that lab. In-group is not Fronterization. Bias is not Unification. The handshake is how little it takes to close. The jobs remain different. Psychology's second plate is a contact, not a theory of the self imported wholesale.
