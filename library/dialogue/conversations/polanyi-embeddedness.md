## They say

Karl Polanyi's *Great Transformation* (1944) treated land, labor, and money as fictitious commodities: they were not made for the market, and a market that treats them as if they were will wreck the substance of society. The double movement is the portable claim — marketization, then a push to protect life from it. Embeddedness is the name: economy inside society, not society inside economy.

A reader who has Tönnies and Marx already expects Polanyi as the third classic of "the market is not the whole."

## Conciliatorics' view

Mixed physiology is organic longing and instrumental longing together, not Gesellschaft winning. Polanyi's fictitious commodities are nearby as an economic-historical fact: living stuff (land, labor) forced to behave as a made tool. Ossification in the technologism/scientism key can rhyme with a market that claims to be the ground.

EIC does not take up the gold standard, Speenhamland, or the double movement as Dex ids. The contact is the embedding, not a history of the nineteenth century.

## Where they meet

Tönnies' two glues, Marx's reversal, Hayek's market knowledge, and Polanyi's embedding can sit together as a family of arguments about whether the made apparatus is allowed to be the ground. They do not agree. A reader of political economy expects this plate.

## Where they part

Polanyi is writing a historical critique of market society. Conciliatorics is not that critique, and it will not make "embeddedness" an operator. Fictitious commodity is not Medium. Double movement is not Conciliation Pathway. The handshake is the refusal of the market as ground. The jobs remain different.
