## They say

Ibn Khaldun's *Muqaddimah* (1377) watched group feeling — *asabiyyah* — found a dynasty on the margins, then thin as luxury, urbanity, and hired arms took over, until a new group feeling replaced it. The cycle is the portable claim: solidarity makes power; power unmakes solidarity. A reader who has Weber, Durkheim, and Schumpeter already expects this plate as a classical cycle of rise and ossification.

## Conciliatorics' view

Organic longing wants to last as a living group. Ossification is an edification that has begun to run the life. The Conciliation Pathway is succession that serves a larger function. Khaldun's cycle is nearby as a historical-political fact: asabiyyah founds, luxury thins, another group arrives. It is not a translation. Asabiyyah is not Organic Physiology. The dynasty's end is not automatically conciliation — it can be replacement by another unification.

## Where they meet

Both describe a living glue that can found a form and then be spent by the form. Durkheim's solidarities, Weber's cage, Schumpeter's destruction, and Khaldun's turn can sit together. Islamic thought as a station exists because this contact is real, not as a gesture.

## Where they part

Ibn Khaldun is writing a science of civilization, with Bedouin and sedentary as types, and a theology in the background. Conciliatorics is not that science. Asabiyyah is not Community. Luxury is not Perdurability, even when they travel together. The handshake is the dynastic turn. The jobs remain different. Do not import the *Muqaddimah* as a fifth parcel.
