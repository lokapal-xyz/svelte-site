One of the main obstacles for a <plain>community</plain> to actively choose Conciliation Synthesis over Unification is that the more relevant a systemic issue is, the higher the resistance of that <plain>community</plain> to embrace the requirements of Conciliation. In other to iterate a functional status, you must let go of Perdurability. And if you do, there's no guaranty that the other members of that system will reciprocate that <plain>acceptance</plain>.
